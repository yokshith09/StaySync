Account

Sign in to
place an order.

Orders, order history and the fulfilment desk are tied to an account. Your email address is your sign-in. Passwords are hashed with salted scrypt and never stored or logged in readable form; the session lives in an HttpOnly cookie.

  • Salted scrypt hashing, constant-time comparison
  • Session cookie is HttpOnly — no token in browser storage
  • Five failed attempts locks an account for fifteen minutes
  • No password, token or email ever reaches the logs
